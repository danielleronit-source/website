If this feels familiar

You want to let go. But part of you keeps watching.

Maybe you love the idea of dancing, but once you start, your attention goes to how you look. You compare yourself, wonder what to do with your body, or feel you need to move a certain way to get it right.

Permission to Dance makes space to explore what’s underneath that self-consciousness, and to discover movement that feels good to you. A chance to feel, express, and enjoy being in your body.

The two-day experience

Work with what holds you back.
Explore what freedom feels like.

Day one · Subconscious work

Meet the blocks beneath the movement.

We explore the subconscious beliefs and emotional patterns that can make it hard to feel free on the dance floor.

That might include fear of being judged, comparing yourself to others, feeling awkward, or the sense that you need to look a certain way. There’s room to meet whatever shows up for you.

Day two · Embodiment

Find your way into your body.

We bring the work into movement, using embodiment practices and tools to help you connect with sensation, notice what feels good, and follow your own rhythm.

The focus is on becoming more comfortable dancing, expressing yourself, and enjoying the experience from within.

Your own rhythm

For you, if you’d love to...

  • Feel less caught in how you look. Bring more of your attention to how movement feels.
  • Explore the beliefs that keep you holding back. Get curious about self-consciousness, judgment, or the pressure to perform.
  • Build a more comfortable connection with your body. Learn tools you can return to when you feel in your head.
  • Give yourself more room to enjoy dancing. Move in a way that feels natural and expressive to you.

Space to explore

Your space. Your body. Your pace.

Both sessions take place on Zoom, so you can join from the privacy of your own home. There’s room to explore this work in a familiar space, without needing to step onto a public dance floor.
