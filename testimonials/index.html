From people I’ve worked with

Everyone’s process is different, but many people notice meaningful changes in how they feel, react, and relate to the issue they came in with.

It's been a grounding and supportive experience so far, and I'm looking forward to continuing."

— Anonymous

"Before working with Danielle, my relationship with money was quite difficult and connected to a lot of negative thoughts and feelings.

Already after the first week, I noticed a real shift. I started to feel so much more ease around money, and that heaviness I had carried before was no longer there.

After working with Danielle, I feel much freer when it comes to money. I have more trust, and suddenly so many different opportunities have opened up that are bringing more money into my life. It feels like something has really shifted.

I think this work can really change the way we relate to certain topics. It can shift how we feel about something, how we experience it, and the perspective we have around it. For me, it created much more ease around money. And I feel that when this inner relationship changes, we also become more open to attracting what previously felt difficult or out of reach."

— Maureen

"I came to work with Danielle because I was looking for an approach that focused specifically on trauma without having to talk through everything from the beginning again. I was tired of psychological therapy where I had to tell my life story each time, remember everything, and leave feeling drained and sad.

With Danielle, it’s the opposite. I’ve already had three sessions with her, and each time I leave feeling energized and stronger, even though the session itself is complex and touches on sensitive, deep parts of my life.

I feel that the work has helped me connect with the intuition in my throat. I’ve learned that when my throat hurts, there’s something I’m not saying. After reflecting on it, when I express it to my partner or during a session with Danielle, I feel relief.

What’s special about IEMT is that the eye movements bypass my mind’s defense mechanisms. They help me feel more deeply, connect with pain and intuition, remember things from the past, and see them from a new perspective of acceptance and awareness.

The most powerful part is that I’m learning to feel, with pain and authenticity. Little by little, I’m able to let go of the defense mechanisms that were with me throughout my childhood. In my sessions with Danielle, I feel that she acknowledges and holds space for my pain and whatever comes up. Her presence is an essential part of the process. She is lovely and warm, and her energy is wonderful and sensitive.

I’m still in the process and have a journey ahead of me, but I definitely plan to continue working with Danielle for some time."

— Dana
