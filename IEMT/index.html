How it works

A gentle hybrid approach for old patterns held in the mind and body.

My private sessions are not just IEMT. I combine guided eye movements, focused questions, IFS-inspired parts work, inner-child healing, somatic awareness, and nervous-system support.

Through the eye movements, we can access memories and associations in the subconscious mind while also helping the body release the emotional charge connected to them. We keep using the eye movements so you stay connected to what is arising underneath the surface, without needing to force or explain everything.

Sometimes, as we work, we meet the inner child or a younger part of you that has been carrying fear, pain, shame, or unmet needs. We give that part a voice and gently offer what it needed then, whether that is safety, love, acceptance, protection, or being taken out of the situation.

For some people, this can also feel like retrieving a fragmented part of the soul. When a part of us has felt separated, exiled, or frozen in the past, we gently bring that soul essence back into connection. That part can receive safety, a new role, and a place within you again, so its energy can return and reintegrate.

Many people who have never been able to access their inner child are surprised that this is the first time they can truly meet that part of themselves. They often say they did not understand how this kind of healing could work until they experienced it in the session.

You stay fully awake, present, and in control the whole time. There is no pressure to relive old stories. We simply follow what your subconscious mind and body bring forward, at a pace that feels safe and manageable.

A few questions before messaging

You can ask me anything before deciding.

What exactly is IEMT and will I have to relive my trauma?

IEMT uses gentle eye movements and focused questions to reduce a memory's emotional charge. You do not have to retell every painful detail. Many clients are surprised by how safely feelings can release without feeling overwhelmed.

Is this safe? Will I feel uncomfortable during the session?

Yes. Safety and comfort are top priorities. Sessions are held gently and at your pace. You remain fully conscious and in control throughout, and I always make sure you feel secure before proceeding.

What can I work on in an IEMT session?

IEMT can be used to work with many different emotional patterns and automatic reactions, including:

  • Anxiety, fear, or emotional triggers
  • Difficult or distressing memories
  • Repetitive or obsessive thoughts
  • Sleep difficulties or insomnia connected to stress, anxiety, or emotional activation
  • Procrastination and avoidance
  • Low confidence or fear of judgment
  • Feeling blocked, frozen, or self-conscious when speaking a second language
  • Relationship patterns
  • Feelings of shame, guilt, anger, or rejection
  • Feeling emotionally stuck
  • Reactions that feel stronger than the present situation
  • Patterns you understand logically but still struggle to change

You do not need to have experienced a major traumatic event to benefit from this work.

Do I need to know where the problem started?

No. You do not need to know why you feel the way you do or which memory originally created the pattern.

We can begin with what is happening in your life now, such as an emotion, reaction, fear, thought, physical sensation, or recurring situation.

As we work, relevant memories and connections may naturally emerge. Sometimes these are experiences you had not consciously connected to the current issue.

How do I know if I need IEMT, IFS, or somatic work versus standard therapy?

This method can support people who feel stuck in recurring patterns, such as phobias, anxiety, identity issues, repeating reactions, or emotional loops where talk therapy alone has not helped enough.

It can often be a faster way to release emotional imprints because we are working directly with the memory, the body, the nervous system, and the parts of you connected to the pattern.

You can combine this work with other therapies, or experience it as a stand-alone coaching process. We can discuss your goals and see if it feels right in a private WhatsApp chat.

Can IEMT be done online?

Yes. IEMT can be done online through Zoom.

For an online session, you will need:

  • A computer rather than a phone
  • A stable internet connection
  • A quiet and private space
  • A camera positioned at eye level
  • Enough lighting for me to clearly see your face and eye movements
How many sessions will I need?

Results vary. Some people feel noticeably lighter after just one to three sessions. Others continue for a few more sessions to work with different issues or deeper layers.

We will set goals together and move at a pace you feel comfortable with. Many clients say progress feels rapid because IEMT targets the root emotional imprint rather than only talking about the story around it.

Is IEMT hypnosis?

No. IEMT is not hypnosis.

You remain awake, aware, and in control throughout the entire session. You can speak, ask questions, pause, or stop at any time.

The eye movements are used while you consciously notice certain emotions, memories, thoughts, or physical sensations. You will remember the session and everything that happens during it.
