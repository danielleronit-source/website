Deepen your self-trust. Find peace within. Follow the path that’s true to you.

Explore the patterns that keep you seeking relief through validation, distraction, or familiar habits, and build tools to feel more grounded within. When the path you’re choosing feels unfamiliar.

Maybe you’ve left a job, ended a relationship, or begun making choices that look different from what you were taught. You can feel that something more is possible, and still feel scared of what comes next.

You may notice how much your sense of being okay depends on what’s happening around you: someone’s approval, a busy schedule, something exciting to look forward to, or a familiar habit that helps you switch off. When those things aren’t there, it can be hard to feel settled or hear what you actually want.

Inner Source is about building a place within yourself that you can return to. Through working with your patterns and developing practical tools, you learn to meet your needs, hear your own direction, and find more peace in the life you’re living now.

Inner Source may be for you if...

  • You’re navigating change—or ready for a different relationship with yourself. You may be choosing a new direction, or want to feel more settled within the life you already have.
  • You want to trust your own path. Your choices may be unconventional, and you’re learning to hear yourself beyond other people’s expectations or approval.
  • You recognize the urge to keep seeking. Reassurance, achievement, stimulation, or familiar habits can bring relief, but you want a more lasting sense of being okay within yourself.
  • You want tools to come back to yourself. Ways to meet emotions, understand your nervous system, reconnect with your body, and hear your intuition.
  • You want your choices to come from what feels true to you. To find more confidence, enjoyment, and freedom as you move toward what you actually want.

A steadier foundation for the life you’re moving toward.

When peace depends on life going a certain way, it can feel as though you’re always waiting for the next thing to finally feel good. We explore what it means to find more of that steadiness, contentment, and confidence within yourself.

That begins with noticing what’s happening inside: what you’re reaching for, what feeling you want to change, and what you may need underneath it. You develop ways to be with yourself, work with the pattern, and return to your center.

From that place, there’s more room to choose. To listen to your intuition, recognize what matters to you, and take action from your own truth. You can enjoy connection, support, and the world around you while developing a steadier place to return to within.

From external regulation to inner stability

Kyle came into Inner Source wanting to understand why it was so difficult to simply slow down and feel at peace.

“I signed up for this program to figure out how to achieve calm and peace without seeking external things. What it comes down to is being at peace on my own.”

He noticed how often his nervous system was looking for something outside of himself: nicotine, caffeine, stimulation, productivity, excitement. Even rest could create stress.

“There's this thought process built into me right now that when I'm not being ‘productive’ I'm wasting time, and so it stresses me out to move more slowly, which is what I truly need.”

By the end of the process, the challenges in his life had not disappeared, but his relationship to them had changed.

“The challenges haven't really changed. I just feel like I'm much better equipped to approach them from a spot of confidence.”

“I just feel more settled into me.”

Inner Source is about becoming more capable of meeting life as it unfolds.

What we work with inside Inner Source

Everyone brings their own experiences. In the group, you’ll explore how these themes show up in your life.

Our work may include:

Your nervous system

Recognize what happens when you feel activated, overwhelmed, shut down, restless, or anxious. Explore what your system needs and practice ways to return to a more grounded place.

Subconscious patterns

Use IEMT and guided exploration to work with the memories, feelings, images, and beliefs underneath a present-day pattern, including reactions that don’t make sense to the rational mind.

External validation

Explore what happens when reassurance or approval isn’t available. Develop a stronger connection to your own experience, even when someone else sees things differently.

Productivity, rest, and feeling enough

Explore the need to keep doing or achieving to feel good about yourself. Practice making room for rest, quiet, and ordinary moments without always needing to earn them.

The habits you reach for

Bring curiosity to scrolling, constant plans, substances, or other familiar ways of finding relief. Understand what they give you and build more choice in how you respond to the needs underneath.

Peace and happiness within

Explore what helps you feel connected, present, and at ease with yourself. Make space for enjoyment and contentment in everyday life, including when there’s nothing to achieve or prove.

Identity

Explore who you are beyond a job, relationship, or role. Make space to discover what matters to you now, rather than only following the version of yourself you’ve learned to be.

Your truth, intuition, and direction

Learn to hear your own needs and values beneath fear, expectations, and the pressure to do what you “should.” Build trust in your ability to choose, adjust, and find your way.

How we work

Inner Source combines several approaches I have trained in and use within my practice, including:

IEMT / eye movement work

to work with emotionally charged memories, subconscious associations, beliefs, and recurring emotional patterns.

Nervous system and somatic work

to become more aware of what is happening in your body and develop a greater capacity to stay present with your experience.

Parts work and inner-child work

to understand the different parts of you that may be protecting, reacting, seeking, avoiding, or holding older emotional experiences.

Reflection and inquiry

to identify the beliefs and patterns shaping the way you relate to yourself and your life.

Practical integration

to build tools you can use between sessions: noticing your needs, working with emotions, listening inward, and making choices that reflect what matters to you.

You don’t need another version of yourself to perform.

There may already be a picture in your mind of who you should be: calm, confident, productive, secure. Here, there’s room for the parts of you that feel afraid, seek approval, or reach for something to change how you feel.

As you understand those parts and what they need, you can begin to meet yourself with more compassion and choice. Coming back to yourself is a relationship you build: listening, responding, and learning to trust your own experience.

What may begin to change

You may notice that you:

  • Notice when you’re seeking relief or validation, with more choice about how you respond.
  • Have ways to meet difficult emotions and return to your center.
  • Feel more comfortable with rest, quiet, and your own company.
  • Find more enjoyment and contentment in ordinary moments.
  • Hear your intuition and recognize what feels true to you.
  • Make decisions with more self-trust and less pressure to please or prove yourself.
  • Take steps toward what you want, even when there’s uncertainty.
  • Feel more settled in who you are as life changes around you.

These shifts can unfold gradually as you bring the practices into your life and build a relationship with yourself you can rely on.
