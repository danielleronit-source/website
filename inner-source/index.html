Inner Source · A 12-week private process

Build the inner stability to trust yourself, regulate your emotions, and move through life without depending on the outside world to make you feel okay.

A 12-week private process for becoming more grounded in yourself, understanding the patterns that keep pulling you out of yourself, and developing the tools to meet life with more calm, confidence, and self-trust.

Book a fit call

You can be doing well in life and still not feel fully at peace within yourself.

Maybe you are capable, self-aware, and already doing a lot of inner work.

But you still notice yourself reaching outside of yourself when something feels uncomfortable.

You need to stay busy.

You reach for stimulation.

You overthink.

You look to other people for reassurance.

You struggle to slow down without feeling guilty.

A relationship ending, a career change, uncertainty, rejection, or simply having too much time alone can suddenly shake your sense of who you are.

And even when you intellectually understand your patterns, that does not necessarily mean you know how to shift them when they are happening.

Inner Source is about changing that relationship with yourself.

Not by trying to become a person who is never anxious, triggered, insecure, overwhelmed, or affected by life.

But by developing the capacity to stay connected to yourself when those things arise.

Inner Source may be for you if...

  • You find yourself relying on relationships, achievement, productivity, substances, stimulation, excitement, or other people's opinions to change how you feel.
  • You have a difficult time slowing down because part of you believes that if you are not being productive, you are wasting time.
  • You understand many of your patterns intellectually, but still find yourself repeating them.
  • You have been through a major life change and feel like you are trying to find yourself again.
  • You second-guess your decisions or look outside yourself for confirmation that you are doing the right thing.
  • You want to feel more confident in who you are without needing everyone around you to understand or approve of you.
  • You know that your nervous system plays a role in how you respond to life, but you want practical tools for actually working with it.
  • You are tired of constantly trying to change your external circumstances in order to finally feel okay internally.
  • You want to be able to experience an ordinary, quiet day without feeling like something is missing.

And ultimately, you want to know:

Can I be okay with myself, even when life isn't giving me the reassurance, excitement, certainty, or validation I'm used to reaching for?

The work is not about becoming unaffected by life.

Life will still happen.

Relationships change.

People disappoint us.

Plans fall apart.

We get triggered.

Old fears resurface.

There are periods when we feel uncertain about who we are or where we are going.

The goal of Inner Source is not to create a life where none of that happens.

The goal is to change who you are in relationship to what happens.

To notice what is happening inside of you before automatically reacting.

To understand where certain emotional patterns come from.

To regulate rather than immediately reaching outside yourself for relief.

To build trust in your ability to handle what comes up.

And to develop a stronger internal reference point for your decisions, your identity, and the way you want to live.

From external regulation to inner stability

Kyle came into Inner Source wanting to understand why it was so difficult to simply slow down and feel at peace.

“I signed up for this program to figure out how to achieve calm and peace without seeking external things. What it comes down to is being at peace on my own.”

He noticed how often his nervous system was looking for something outside of himself: nicotine, caffeine, stimulation, productivity, excitement.

Even rest could create stress.

“There's this thought process built into me right now that when I'm not being ‘productive’ I'm wasting time, and so it stresses me out to move more slowly, which is what I truly need.”

By the end of the process, the challenges in his life had not magically disappeared.

But his relationship to them had changed.

“The challenges haven't really changed. I just feel like I'm much better equipped to approach them from a spot of confidence.”

“I just feel more settled into me.”

That is a large part of what Inner Source is about.

Not controlling life.

Becoming more capable of meeting it.

That relationship with yourself is something you build.

What we work with inside Inner Source

Every person's patterns are different, so the process is personalized.

But throughout our work, we may explore:

Your nervous system

Learning to recognize what happens inside of you when you become activated, overwhelmed, shut down, restless, anxious, or emotionally triggered.

Instead of only asking, “How do I make this feeling go away?” we begin understanding what your system is actually doing and what it needs.

Subconscious patterns

Some of our strongest reactions make very little sense when we look at them only through the rational mind.

Using IEMT and subconscious work, we follow the thoughts, memories, emotions, sensations, images, and beliefs that arise underneath a present-day pattern.

Often, what appears to be one issue leads somewhere much deeper.

External validation

Whose opinion determines whether you feel confident?

What happens when someone disapproves of you?

What happens when you do not receive reassurance?

What would it mean to trust your own experience even when somebody else sees things differently?

Your relationship with productivity and rest

For some people, slowing down does not actually feel relaxing.

It feels unsafe.

Unproductive.

Wasteful.

Uncomfortable.

We explore the beliefs and nervous system patterns underneath the constant need to do, achieve, fix, distract, or move.

The ways you regulate yourself externally

This does not mean judging the things you reach for.

It means becoming curious about them.

Food.

Scrolling.

Work.

Relationships.

Substances.

Attention.

Exercise.

Excitement.

Constant plans.

Why does your system reach for them?

What feeling are you trying to create or avoid?

And what happens when you begin developing more ways of meeting those needs internally?

Identity

Who are you when a relationship ends?

When your career changes?

When somebody stops choosing you?

When you stop performing the version of yourself you've been rewarded for being?

We begin separating who you actually are from the roles, expectations, relationships, and identities you have learned to attach yourself to.

Self-trust

Self-trust is not believing you will always make the perfect decision.

It is knowing that even when you don't, you can meet yourself there.

You can listen.

You can adjust.

You can take responsibility.

You can choose again.

How we work

Inner Source combines several approaches I have trained in and use within my practice, including:

IEMT / eye movement work

to work with emotionally charged memories, subconscious associations, beliefs, and recurring emotional patterns.

Nervous system and somatic work

to become more aware of what is happening in your body and develop a greater capacity to stay present with your experience.

Parts work and inner-child work

to understand the different parts of you that may be protecting, reacting, seeking, avoiding, or holding older emotional experiences.

Reflection and inquiry

to identify the beliefs and patterns shaping the way you relate to yourself and your life.

Practical integration

so the work does not only happen during our sessions, but starts changing the way you respond to your actual life.

You do not need another version of yourself to perform.

Calm.

Confident.

Disciplined.

Secure.

Productive.

Emotionally regulated.
