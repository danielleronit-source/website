My method

I combine IEMT with Internal Family Systems.

IEMT, Integral Eye Movement Technique, uses guided eye movements, focused questions, and nervous system regulation to work with experiences from the past that are still affecting us today.

Internal Family Systems, also known as IFS, is a parts-work approach. It helps us understand the different parts inside us, including protector parts, wounded parts, and the inner child.

In my sessions, I bring these two worlds together. We use IEMT to gently bypass the protector parts that often make inner child work feel blocked, confusing, or impossible to access.

This allows us to reach the younger part of you that is carrying the emotion, belief, fear, or survival strategy underneath the pattern, often much faster than talking alone.

For people who struggle to access their inner child

We go beneath the protection.

Many people try inner child work and feel nothing, see nothing, or meet a wall inside. That wall is often not resistance. It is protection.

Protector parts are the parts of us that learned to keep us safe. They may shut us down, distract us, make us overthink, avoid, numb, control, perform, or disconnect from feeling.

By combining IEMT with IFS, we do not fight those protectors. We work with the nervous system in a way that helps soften their grip, so the younger wounded part can finally be reached with more ease.

Clients who have said they could never connect with their inner child often find themselves accessing that part already in the first session, with powerful emotional clarity and relief.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions you might have before booking

What is the difference between EMDR, IEMT, and this work?

EMDR and IEMT are often compared because they both use eye movements, but they are very different methods.

EMDR usually focuses on a specific traumatic or distressing memory. During the process, you bring the memory to mind and work with the emotions, thoughts, beliefs, and physical sensations connected to it. EMDR may use eye movements, alternating hand taps, or sounds.

IEMT can also begin with a specific memory, but it may begin with an emotion, reaction, repeating pattern, or area where you feel stuck.

During IEMT, several connected memories and associations may naturally come up. Depending on the situation, this can bring greater clarity, self-understanding, or compassion. In other situations, it may create more distance from the memory, so it no longer has the same emotional effect in the present.

In my work, I combine IEMT with Internal Family Systems, parts work, and inner child work. That means we are not only reducing emotional intensity. We are also listening to the parts of you that formed around the experience, including the protectors and the younger part they have been trying to protect.

You do not need to retell the entire story or share every detail out loud. We work with what your system brings forward, at a pace that feels safe and manageable.

Do I need to know where the problem started?

No. You do not need to know why you feel the way you do or which memory originally created the pattern.

We can begin with what is happening in your life now, such as an emotion, reaction, fear, thought, physical sensation, or recurring situation.

As we work, relevant memories, inner child parts, and connections may naturally emerge. Sometimes these are experiences you had not consciously connected to the current issue.

What is Internal Family Systems?

Internal Family Systems, or IFS, is a way of understanding the inner world through parts. One part of you may want to move forward, while another part may feel afraid, protective, angry, numb, or young.

In parts work, we do not try to get rid of any part. We listen to what each part is trying to protect, what it learned, and what it needs now.

Inner child work is part of this process. It helps us connect with younger parts of ourselves that may still be holding fear, pain, loneliness, shame, or unmet needs from the past.

What if I cannot access my inner child?

That is very common. Many people arrive feeling like they have tried inner child work before and nothing happened.

Often, the reason is that protector parts are doing their job. They may block emotion, create mental noise, keep you analyzing, or make it feel impossible to go deeper.

By using IEMT alongside parts work, we can often move beneath those protective layers more gently and access the younger part with less struggle.

Is this hypnosis?

No. This is not hypnosis.

You remain awake, aware, and in control throughout the entire session. You can speak, ask questions, pause, or stop at any time.

The eye movements and parts work are used while you consciously notice emotions, memories, thoughts, physical sensations, and inner parts. You will remember the session and everything that happens during it.
