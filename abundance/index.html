Abundance · An 8-week women's journey

Open Yourself to More.

An 8-week women's journey to release the beliefs and emotional patterns limiting your capacity to receive, and create more space for abundance, confidence, and possibility.

Explore the next cohort

A recurring group journey, with new cohorts throughout the year.

Who this journey is for

A space to meet what’s been holding you back.

You may feel ready for more, while noticing that old beliefs about money, worthiness, or receiving still shape what feels possible. This journey is an invitation to explore those patterns in connection with other women.

  • You notice guilt, discomfort, or fear around money or receiving.
  • You find it easier to give than to let yourself be supported.
  • You want to explore the connection between self-worth and what you allow into your life.
  • You’re drawn to reflection and emotional work in a group of women.

The journey

What we’ll explore over 8 weeks

These are the themes of our journey, rather than a fixed week-by-week curriculum. There is space to reflect on what they mean in your own life.

Your relationship with money

Explore the beliefs and feelings you carry about money, including what you learned about having, wanting, and receiving.

Self-worth and receiving

Notice where your sense of worth shapes what you feel allowed to ask for, accept, or enjoy.

The patterns beneath the surface

Bring attention to subconscious emotional patterns that may keep familiar limits in place.

Abundance beyond money

Make room to explore support, connection, confidence, and possibility alongside your relationship with money.

My approach

Reflection, emotional work, and connection.

My approach brings together guided reflection, emotional release work, and group connection. We explore the relationship between what you believe, what you feel, and the patterns that show up in everyday life.

This is a space for curiosity, honesty, and meeting yourself where you are.

What you may take away

Something to carry into everyday life.

The intention is to support a more conscious relationship with money, receiving, and your own worth. What you take away will be personal to you.

  • A chance to recognize beliefs and emotional patterns you want to relate to differently.
  • Space to explore what receiving support and abundance could look like for you.
  • Reflections to return to as you notice these themes in everyday life.
  • An experience of exploring these questions alongside other women.

This is an invitation to explore, rather than a promise of a particular emotional or financial outcome.
