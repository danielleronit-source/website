My Journey Into Abundance & Subconscious Healing

I first became fascinated by the subconscious mind when I was 18. I was reading Joe Dispenza, and the idea stuck with me: what happens inside of us shapes so much of what we experience on the outside.

I loved that idea. I just had no idea how to actually live it.

For years, I looked for happiness outside of myself. After the army, I was living in Tel Aviv, going out a lot, drinking, and always searching for something more.

Then I went to my first conscious festival, completely without drugs or alcohol, and experienced a kind of joy, connection, and aliveness I didn't know was possible.

It changed the direction of my life.

I stopped drinking and began traveling, studying, and immersing myself in healing work, with one question at the center of it all:

How do I create the peace, happiness, and abundance I'm looking for from within?

I ended up spending more than five years living in conscious communities, mostly in Thailand, but also in places like Peru, Mexico, and Bali.

Over those years, I explored countless tools, trainings, retreats, and healing modalities. Many helped me understand and manage my emotions, but I was still looking for something that could work more directly with the deeper patterns underneath them.

That's what I eventually found in IEMT (Integral Eye Movement Therapy).

After experiencing a profound shift through IEMT in my own life, I became a certified practitioner and began combining it with somatic work, inner child work, and other tools I'd learned.

But there was another journey unfolding alongside all of this.

My relationship with money, work, and receiving.

From Hustling to Trusting

I've been on the entrepreneurial path for around seven years.

And it has been quite a journey.

I've experienced the excitement of starting something new, the fear of putting myself out there, the uncertainty of not knowing when the next client would come, and periods when I barely had enough money to get by.

I started out teaching English online, helping people develop confidence in speaking. Even then, I was drawn to the deeper emotional patterns underneath their struggles, especially perfectionism and the fear of making mistakes.

Over time, I felt called toward different work.

Women's circles. Workshops. Retreats. Emotional healing.

And every time I felt called to take another step, fear came up.

I remember how vulnerable it felt to facilitate my first women's circles. I wasn't just bringing women together to talk. I was creating entire experiences with movement, meditation, emotional exploration, and connection.

It meant so much to me that putting myself out there felt terrifying.

But I did it anyway.

And then I held my first retreat. My first longer group program. Eventually, I began offering IEMT sessions and developing my own approach to this work.

Little by little, I was creating a life and business that felt more and more aligned with who I was.

But there was still one pattern I hadn't been able to let go of.

The belief that I had to work incredibly hard to make money.

For years, my relationship with earning was built around doing.

Doing more. Trying harder. Finding another strategy. Launching another offer.

And when money felt uncertain, I pushed even harder.

Eventually, I reached a turning point.

I knew deep down that English coaching was no longer the work I wanted to be doing. My heart was in emotional healing and women's empowerment.

But people around me encouraged me to keep teaching English for financial stability while I built my other work.

And that made sense.

So I tried.

But things that had worked for years suddenly stopped working. I remember having four women sign up for an English group program and then cancel, all within the same week.

I was spending money on advertising, trying to make things happen, and feeling increasingly disconnected from what I really wanted.

Eventually, I decided to let it go.

And suddenly, I had no steady income and no savings to fall back on.

I didn't know how I was going to pay my rent.

For weeks, I experienced a level of fear and tension that I can only describe as feeling like I was dying.

Every part of me wanted to do more.

Find another way to make money. Launch something. Hustle harder.

Because that's what I had always done.

Until one day, I asked myself:

What's the one thing I haven't tried yet?

And the answer came.

Stop.

Stop trying to control everything.

Stop believing I had to make everything happen by myself.

Let go, and allow God to show me that I was supported.

It was one of the scariest things I'd ever done.

At that time, I was already using IEMT on myself, together with writing, somatic practices, and inner child work.

During one of those sessions, I connected with a very young part of myself that was carrying this overwhelming fear of not being safe.

Suddenly, the intensity of what I was feeling made more sense.

It wasn't only about the money in my bank account.

There was something much deeper inside of me that associated not having enough with danger.

As I worked with that part of myself, something began to change.

For the first time, I started to feel safe without needing my external circumstances to change first.

I still didn't know how I would pay my rent.

I still didn't have financial security.

But I felt calmer.

I stopped trying to force everything.

I continued taking the steps that felt aligned, offering my sessions and showing up for the work I loved, but I let go of the constant pressure to make things happen.

And somehow, I paid my rent that month.

Clients started coming. Opportunities started appearing. I found myself able to pay for things without the same constant panic I'd been carrying.

I began experiencing something I'd heard people talk about for years, but had never really understood.

What it feels like to receive.

To feel supported.

To do the work I love without feeling like I have to exhaust myself to earn money.

To take action from inspiration and alignment rather than fear.

And to trust that I don't have to figure everything out alone.

This has become one of the most profound shifts of my life.

It doesn't mean I never feel afraid, or that I never need to put in effort.

It means that when I notice myself forcing, pushing, or falling back into that old fear, I have a different way of meeting it.

And I keep choosing a different relationship with money, work, and receiving.

Why I Created The Abundance Container

This program is incredibly close to my heart.

Because I know what it's like to have big dreams and feel afraid to pursue them.

I know what it's like to love your work and still feel uncomfortable receiving money for it.

I know what it's like to believe you have to keep doing more, working harder, and figuring everything out yourself.

And I've experienced how much can change when we begin working with the deeper emotional patterns underneath those beliefs.

I believe there is so much more available to us than we often allow ourselves to imagine.

More money. More love. More connection. More freedom. More fulfillment.

I believe God wants to bless us abundantly, and that part of our journey is learning to become vessels that can receive and hold those blessings.

For me, feminine abundance is deeply connected to that capacity to receive.

It's about listening to our inner guidance, honoring what feels aligned, releasing the fears that keep us small, and learning that we can be supported without constantly living in a state of struggle.

That doesn't mean sitting back and waiting for life to happen.

It means having the courage to follow our dreams, take meaningful action, and trust that we don't have to force every outcome.

That's what The Abundance Container is about.

Together, we explore the subconscious beliefs, emotional memories, and protective parts of ourselves that may be influencing our relationship with money, worthiness, and receiving.

We use IEMT and other emotional exploration practices to work with those patterns and create space for new ways of experiencing ourselves and our lives.

My hope is that each woman leaves feeling lighter, more connected to herself, more confident in what she desires, and more open to the possibilities ahead of her.

Because you're allowed to want more.

You're allowed to dream bigger.

And you're allowed to enjoy the journey of creating a life you truly love.

How the eye movement work unfolds

Deep work, held gently.

I guide the group through themes around money and receiving: what we learned from our parents, our environment, and our life experiences; beliefs like “I have to work hard for money”; and the tension we carry in our bodies.

For example, I might invite you to remember a time when receiving something felt uncomfortable or brought up guilt, or when you were afraid to ask for what you wanted. Then we do the eye movements while connecting with that experience.

As we work, different memories and emotions may surface. The eye movements can bring a relaxed, trance-like quality to the experience, helping us explore beliefs and associations beneath our everyday awareness. We work gently with what emerges, with the intention of releasing old emotional weight and creating space for new ways of feeling and responding.

  • The eye movements can help you drop in quickly, almost like a light trance state, without hypnosis.
  • Instead of talking around the feeling, we work with it directly and gently.
  • As the eyes keep moving, the emotional weight connected to a memory or pattern can begin to release in the same moment.

This can touch very deep material, including past trauma. Emotions may come up; the aim is to meet them gently while staying connected to the present, rather than becoming flooded. Every person’s experience is different, and you can pause at any time.

The power of a group

Your own process. A circle beside you.

I developed my own way of guiding eye movement work in a group, bringing together my IEMT training, somatic work, and parts work. I hold the space with trauma awareness and attention to each woman.

There’s a depth that comes from working together: recognizing yourself in another woman’s experience, feeling less alone, and being witnessed as something shifts. The group has its own power, while each of you moves through your own process.

Before you apply

Come with a foundation in inner work.

This program is for women who already have experience with personal development or emotional healing and feel ready to explore more deeply. It is not an introduction to inner work.

  • You recognize repeating patterns around money, receiving, or following your dreams.
  • You have ways to ground yourself and care for your emotions between sessions.
  • You’re willing to make space for integration and seek individual support when needed.

Give yourself space afterward.

Even when the work feels gentle, touching deep material can leave you feeling tender. Please leave yourself time to rest and integrate. If you need more support, you can book a separate 1:1 session with me.

What you’ll need for each session

A computer with your camera at eye level, reliable internet, and a quiet, private space for the full 90 minutes. Please arrive with time and attention for yourself.
