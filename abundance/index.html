Welcome to the Abundance Container

You know there’s more available to you.

You have a vision for your work and your life. Maybe you’re already running a business, preparing to launch one, or ready to take what you’ve built further. You want to earn more, reach more people, and enjoy the freedom your work can create.

Yet taking the next step can bring things up: doubt about what you charge, discomfort being visible, guilt around wanting more, or the feeling that earning more means working even harder. You may recognize these patterns and still find yourself meeting the same ceiling.

We work with the deeper subconscious patterns shaping your money story. Over eight weeks, we use guided eye movements and body awareness to uncover and work through beliefs about earning, success, and receiving. Some you may already recognize; others may be programs you never knew you were carrying. We meet what comes up along the way, creating room to shift those old patterns and choose differently.

The eye movement work

How this works

We begin with themes around money, receiving, self-worth, and the beliefs we may have inherited from our parents, our upbringing, or society. Through guided eye movements, reflection, writing, and body awareness, we explore what these themes bring up for you—including subconscious patterns you may recognize, and others you may not yet know you carry.

The eye movements can help bring you into a light, trance-like state, where memories and emotions that are usually outside your conscious awareness may begin to surface. We gently connect with these experiences while moving the eyes, helping to soften and release the emotional charge they carry.

Sometimes deep material can emerge, so we work gently, staying connected to your body and the present moment. You're always in control and can pause at any time.

My Journey Into Abundance

My relationship with money has been one of my deepest teachers. Building a business brought me face to face with the beliefs I carried about hard work, safety, and what it means to receive.

From Hustling to Trusting

I’ve been an entrepreneur for about seven years. It’s been quite a ride. The excitement of starting something, the fear of putting myself out there, not knowing when the next client would come, and stretches when I barely had enough to get by.

I started out teaching English online, helping people feel confident speaking. Over time I felt called toward women’s circles, workshops, retreats, and emotional healing. Every step scared me. My first women’s circle felt so vulnerable because it meant so much to me. I did it anyway. Then came my first retreat, my first longer program, and eventually my IEMT sessions.

One pattern stayed stuck. I believed I had to work incredibly hard to make money. When money felt uncertain, I pushed harder.

Then I hit a turning point. I knew English coaching wasn’t the work I wanted anymore. People around me suggested I keep it for stability, and that made sense, so I tried. But things that used to work stopped working. Four women signed up for an English group and cancelled in the same week. I was spending money on ads and feeling more and more disconnected from what I wanted.

So I let it go. Suddenly I had no steady income and no savings. I didn’t know how I’d pay my rent. For weeks the fear in my body felt like dying. Every part of me wanted to hustle harder.

Then one day I asked myself: what’s the one thing I haven’t tried?

The answer was simple. Stop. Stop trying to control everything. Let go, and let God show me that I was supported. It was one of the scariest things I’ve ever done.

Around then I was using IEMT on myself, along with writing and inner child work. In one session I met a very young part of me carrying a huge fear of not being safe. The intensity finally made sense. Somewhere deep inside, not having enough meant danger.

As I worked with that part of me, something shifted. For the first time, I felt safe before anything outside had changed. I still didn’t know how I’d pay my rent. But I felt calmer. I kept showing up for the work I loved and stopped forcing.

And I paid my rent that month. Clients started coming. Opportunities appeared. I got to know what receiving feels like. Doing work I love without exhausting myself, and taking action from inspiration instead of fear.

I still get scared sometimes. When I catch myself pushing, I have a different way to meet it. And I keep choosing a new relationship with money, work, and receiving.

Why I Created The Abundance Container

This program is so close to my heart.

I know what it’s like to have big dreams and feel afraid to go after them. To love your work and still feel awkward receiving money for it. To believe you have to keep doing more and figure it all out alone.

I also know how much can change when we work with the emotional patterns underneath those beliefs.

I believe there’s so much more available to us. More money, love, connection, freedom. I believe God wants to bless us abundantly, and part of our journey is becoming a vessel that can receive and hold it.

In the container, we explore the subconscious beliefs, emotional memories, and learned patterns that shape how we feel about money, worth, and receiving. We use IEMT and other practices to work with them and make room for something new.

My hope is that you leave feeling lighter, closer to yourself, and more open to what’s ahead.

You’re allowed to want more. You’re allowed to dream bigger. And you’re allowed to enjoy creating a life you love.

The themes we explore

Eight weeks to make room for more.

We meet once a week for 90 minutes on Zoom. The themes guide our work, with room for what emerges along the way.

Themes we may explore include:

  • Money and receiving—what comes up around earning more, charging for your work, asking for what you want, and allowing support.
  • Self-worth and imposter syndrome—trusting the value of your work without needing to prove yourself or be further along.
  • Inherited beliefs about success—the messages you grew up with about money, ambition, hard work, and how much you’re allowed to have.
  • Safety in growth—meeting the fear of uncertainty, responsibility, and what might change when your work becomes more successful.
  • Perfectionism and overworking—the pressure to get everything right or keep doing more before you can earn, rest, or enjoy your success.
  • Visibility and taking the next step—sharing your work, launching your ideas, and moving toward bigger possibilities even when you feel exposed.

The themes are connected and may overlap or return in different ways. We’ll see where the group needs more time as we go.

What can open up

More room to earn, create, and enjoy your life.

The intention is to feel freer in how you approach your work, your income, and your next steps. That can show up in practical choices as well as how you feel within yourself:

  • More trust in your decisions

    Greater clarity around what you want to create, with less second-guessing or looking outside yourself for permission.

  • More ease around money

    Space to work through the shame, fear, or guilt that can come with charging, earning more, and receiving.

  • Courage to put your work out there

    Making room to share an offer, launch an idea, or show up more visibly without waiting to feel perfect.

    Noticing where you hold yourself back and taking steps toward opportunities that fit your vision.

  • Success with space to enjoy it

    Making room for boundaries, rest, support, and pleasure as your work grows.

This is inner work that supports the steps you take in the world. More abundance can mean more money, and also more freedom, purpose, and space to enjoy the life you’re creating.

Is this space for you?

This is for you if...

For women already moving toward a vision, ready to expand their work, income, and sense of what’s possible.

  • You have a vision you’re taking steps toward. You’re building a business, growing one, or preparing to bring your work into the world.
  • You want to earn more without making constant pressure your way of life. You’re ready for more money and opportunity, with room for rest, support, and enjoyment.
  • You notice where you hold yourself back. Charging more, being seen, receiving, or taking your next step brings up familiar doubts or discomfort you want to work through.
  • You’re willing to act on what you discover. You want to trust yourself, follow your own direction, and take steps toward more—while making room for ease and enjoyment along the way.

A foundation for this work

You’ve already done some therapy, coaching, somatic work, or other inner work. You can notice when you’re checking out, have ways to ground yourself and return to your body, and bring practices to support yourself between sessions.

You’re willing to meet emotions such as sadness or anger, recognize when you need to pause, and ask for support. Experience with subconscious work is helpful: an image or feeling may come up before you understand it, and you’re open to exploring it without needing it all to make sense immediately.

This is not for you if...

  • You’re looking for business strategy or a marketing plan. This program focuses on the inner beliefs and emotional patterns that come up as you grow. You bring your own vision and take practical steps toward it.
  • You’re looking for a guaranteed outcome or a quick formula for abundance. There is no fixed path to a particular income, career, or lifestyle. The work asks for curiosity, patience, and a willingness to explore what unfolds.
  • You want change without taking steps outside the sessions. You don’t need to have it all figured out, but this space is for women willing to bring what they discover into their everyday choices and actions.
  • You’re completely new to inner work. Some previous experience with therapy, coaching, somatic work, or emotional healing is needed, along with ways to notice disconnection, ground yourself, and work with emotions between sessions.
  • You’re not ready to explore feelings or sit with some uncertainty. Emotions such as sadness or anger may arise, and an image or feeling may not make sense immediately. You can always pause; some willingness to meet what comes up is part of this work.

Frequently asked questions

About the program & the work.

How does the 8-week program work?

The Abundance Container is an 8-week program for women. We meet once a week for 90-minute Zoom sessions. The next cohort begins in January 2027. The full eight-week program costs ₪1,800 or $555 USD. Join the waitlist for exact dates and signup details.

What if it doesn’t feel right for me?

If after your first session you feel this isn’t for you, just let me know and you’ll receive a full refund, no questions asked.

How do the themes work?

The themes guide our eight sessions around money, receiving, self-worth, visibility, and growth. We explore what these bring up in your own work and life. Themes may overlap or return, because they often share the same roots.

How do we use the eye movements?

I guide you through reflection, writing, and focused eye movements to explore the feelings and subconscious beliefs that surface around money, visibility, and taking your next step.

The eye movements can help bring you into a light, trance-like state, where memories and emotions that are usually outside your conscious awareness may begin to surface. We gently connect with these experiences while moving the eyes, helping to soften and release the emotional charge they carry.

What might I feel during a session?

You may notice memories, emotions, or sensations in your body as we work. Tears can come, and there may be moments of emotional release. You might also experience something quieter. There is no particular response you need to have.

I guide you to stay connected to what you feel, rather than only talking about it. You can move at your own pace and pause whenever you need to.

I like to think of it as defrosting emotions that got stuck in time, while creating space to shift the beliefs we formed from those experiences.

What might I notice after my first session?

Some women tell me they feel lighter afterward, even when they can’t quite explain what has changed. When the work is new, that unfamiliar feeling can bring questions: “Why do I feel different? What just happened?” You don’t have to make sense of everything straight away.

As you become more familiar with the work, you may begin to notice shifts in everyday life: how you respond to a familiar situation, what feels less charged, or how you relate to yourself. Everyone’s experience is different, and there is no set timeline.

What if an image or feeling comes up that doesn’t make sense?

When working with the subconscious, you may notice an image, sensation, feeling, or association without knowing why it’s there. You don’t need to decode it or turn it into a clear story before we can explore it.

We can stay curious about what you notice and how it feels in your body, letting the process unfold at your pace. Sometimes a connection becomes clearer afterward; sometimes you can work with a feeling without finding an explanation. An image doesn’t have to be taken literally or treated as proof of something that happened.

Experience with this kind of exploration is helpful. You’re invited to trust your own pace and boundaries, and you can always pause or say that something doesn’t feel right.

Do I need to know where a pattern began?

You don’t need to arrive with everything figured out. We begin with a theme and the feelings it brings up. Memories or associations may emerge along the way, and we work gently with what is present for you.

What if strong emotions come up?

Sometimes deep material can emerge, so we work gently, staying connected to your body and the present moment. You're always in control and can pause at any time.

You can choose how much to share and move at your own pace. Please listen to what you need and let me know if you need support.

Give yourself space afterward.

Even when the work feels gentle, touching deep material can leave you feeling tender. Please leave yourself time to rest and integrate. If you need more support, you can book a separate 1:1 session with me.
