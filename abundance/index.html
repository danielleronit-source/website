You’re already on your path

You’ve built something you love. You’re earning. You’ve taken brave steps toward a life that feels like yours.

And you can feel there’s more.

Maybe you’d love to raise your prices without that knot in your stomach. Maybe you want money to feel lighter. Maybe you’re tired of thinking every good thing has to be earned with effort.

If that sounds like you, this is for you.

Maybe the next level is about receiving

A lot of us learned that more means more doing. Push harder. Prove yourself. Keep going.

I see it differently. I believe abundance is already on its way to us. My work is helping you grow your capacity to receive it and hold it.

Sometimes one part of you wants more money while another part gets scared of what more would mean. Raising your prices brings up guilt. Ease feels strange because your body is used to working hard for everything.

These patterns live below the surface. When we find them and let them go, there’s room for so much more.

What we’ll explore together

Your money story. The beliefs and memories that shaped how you feel about money.

Worth and receiving. What comes up when you ask for more, raise your prices, or let yourself be supported.

Releasing emotional blocks. We use Integral Eye Movement Therapy (IEMT) to work with feelings that talking alone hasn’t shifted.

Growing your capacity. Receiving and enjoying more money, love, and opportunity without guilt.

Dreaming bigger. Letting yourself want what you really want, and taking real steps toward it.

Money is just one doorway. Many women notice changes in how they receive love, ask for help, and say yes to new opportunities.

How the work feels

I love eye movement work because you don’t have to figure everything out first. Sometimes you know exactly what’s holding you back. Sometimes something surprising comes up in the session. Either way, it gives us a way in.

My first group was 6 women. Several of them told me they felt lighter around money, got more comfortable asking for what they want, and started noticing new opportunities. Everyone’s experience is different.

Hi, I’m Danielle

I’ve always followed my intuition, even when it looked a little crazy from the outside.

Right after college, I knew the nine-to-five and the move to San Francisco wasn’t for me. So I left America and moved to Israel. I joined the army, lived on a kibbutz for a couple of years, and then took a long trip through India, Nepal, and Thailand.

That’s where I found Koh Phangan. People were living so differently there. Slow, in nature, working online, completely free. I thought, I want that too. So during COVID, I moved. I ended up staying five and a half years. This October, I moved back to Jerusalem.

Underneath all of it, my life became about healing.

I grew up with a heavy scarcity mindset. I didn’t believe real joy was possible for me, and I never felt like I belonged. So I went looking for myself, my people, and work that felt good.

It started with teaching English online and helping adults feel confident speaking. Their English was fine. What held them back was perfectionism. Then I began working with women, and that grew into workshops, retreats, and programs.

About a year ago, I found eye movement therapy. I was visiting Israel on October 7th, and I had nightmares for two years afterward. Then when the Iran war began, I was visiting again and couldn’t sleep at all. I booked one IEMT session, and the nightmares stopped. Just like that. I could sleep.

I started using it on myself. Then I blended it with what I’d learned in my trauma awareness training, somatic work, and inner child work, and slowly built my own method. I kept watching people have deep shifts, and fast.

That’s what led me here.

When you follow your dreams, the emotional blocks come up. Of course they do. Dreaming big is brave, and your system needs a way to release what’s keeping you stuck. As we clear that out, we can hold more and more light. Things start to flow with more ease.

That’s what I’ve lived. My outside life started to match my inside work. More joy. More love. Better relationships. More money, from work I love. I’m the happiest I’ve ever been, and I never knew life could feel this good.

I know it’s possible for every woman. Our path to abundance looks different from what we were taught. It doesn’t come from pushing harder or ignoring your body and your inner wisdom. It comes from taking brave steps, even when they don’t make sense yet.

That’s what we do in Dream Bigger.

A small circle

I keep the group to 8 women, so everyone gets real attention.

We meet on Zoom once a week for 90 minutes, for 8 weeks.

You’ll hear another woman say out loud the fear you’ve carried quietly for years. You’ll watch someone give herself permission to ask for more. And you’ll be witnessed doing the same.

Is this for you?

You might love this if:

  • You’re already following your dreams and want to expand
  • You run your own business and want to feel easier about receiving money
  • You notice big feelings or old patterns around money
  • You want more abundance without overworking
  • You’re open to deeper emotional work and willing to care for yourself along the way

You don’t need to have it figured out. Just a willingness to look inward.

Before you join

This is deep work, and big feelings can come up. Please make sure you have ways to look after yourself and extra support if you need it.

I recommend a private 1:1 session with me first, so you can feel IEMT in your own system. It’s optional, but it helps.

The group is for personal growth. It isn’t a replacement for therapy or mental health treatment.

You’ll need: a computer with your camera at eye level, solid internet, and a quiet private space where you won’t be interrupted for the full 90 minutes. Please treat each session like a therapy appointment.
