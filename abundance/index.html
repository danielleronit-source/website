My Journey Into Abundance & Subconscious Healing

I first became fascinated by the subconscious mind when I was 18. I was reading Joe Dispenza, and the idea stuck with me: what happens inside of us shapes so much of what we experience on the outside.

I loved that idea. I just had no idea how to actually live it.

For years, I looked for happiness outside of myself. After the army, I was living in Tel Aviv, going out a lot, drinking, and always searching for something more.

Then I went to my first conscious festival, completely without drugs or alcohol, and experienced a kind of joy, connection, and aliveness I didn't know was possible.

It changed the direction of my life.

I stopped drinking and began traveling, studying, and immersing myself in healing work, with one question at the center of it all:

How do I create the peace, happiness, and abundance I'm looking for from within?

I ended up spending more than five years living in conscious communities, mostly in Thailand, but also in places like Peru, Mexico, and Bali.

Over those years, I explored countless tools, trainings, retreats, and healing modalities. Many helped me understand and manage my emotions, but I was still looking for something that could work more directly with the deeper patterns underneath them.

That's what I eventually found in IEMT (Integral Eye Movement Therapy).

After experiencing a profound shift through IEMT in my own life, I became a certified practitioner and began combining it with somatic work, inner child work, and other tools I'd learned.

But there was another journey unfolding alongside all of this.

My relationship with money, work, and receiving.

From Hustling to Trusting

I’ve been an entrepreneur for about seven years. It’s been quite a ride. The excitement of starting something, the fear of putting myself out there, not knowing when the next client would come, and stretches when I barely had enough to get by.

I started out teaching English online, helping people feel confident speaking. Over time I felt called toward women’s circles, workshops, retreats, and emotional healing. Every step scared me. My first women’s circle felt so vulnerable because it meant so much to me. I did it anyway. Then came my first retreat, my first longer program, and eventually my IEMT sessions.

One pattern stayed stuck. I believed I had to work incredibly hard to make money. When money felt uncertain, I pushed harder.

Then I hit a turning point. I knew English coaching wasn’t the work I wanted anymore. People around me suggested I keep it for stability, and that made sense, so I tried. But things that used to work stopped working. Four women signed up for an English group and cancelled in the same week. I was spending money on ads and feeling more and more disconnected from what I wanted.

So I let it go. Suddenly I had no steady income and no savings. I didn’t know how I’d pay my rent. For weeks the fear in my body felt like dying. Every part of me wanted to hustle harder.

Then one day I asked myself: what’s the one thing I haven’t tried?

The answer was simple. Stop. Stop trying to control everything. Let go, and let God show me that I was supported. It was one of the scariest things I’ve ever done.

Around then I was using IEMT on myself, along with writing and inner child work. In one session I met a very young part of me carrying a huge fear of not being safe. The intensity finally made sense. Somewhere deep inside, not having enough meant danger.

As I worked with that part of me, something shifted. For the first time, I felt safe before anything outside had changed. I still didn’t know how I’d pay my rent. But I felt calmer. I kept showing up for the work I loved and stopped forcing.

And I paid my rent that month. Clients started coming. Opportunities appeared. I got to know what receiving feels like. Doing work I love without exhausting myself, and taking action from inspiration instead of fear.

I still get scared sometimes. When I catch myself pushing, I have a different way to meet it. And I keep choosing a new relationship with money, work, and receiving.

Why I Created The Abundance Container

This program is so close to my heart.

I know what it’s like to have big dreams and feel afraid to go after them. To love your work and still feel awkward receiving money for it. To believe you have to keep doing more and figure it all out alone.

I also know how much can change when we work with the emotional patterns underneath those beliefs.

I believe there’s so much more available to us. More money, love, connection, freedom. I believe God wants to bless us abundantly, and part of our journey is becoming a vessel that can receive and hold it.

In the container, we explore the subconscious beliefs, emotional memories, and protective parts that shape how we feel about money, worth, and receiving. We use IEMT and other practices to work with them and make room for something new.

My hope is that you leave feeling lighter, closer to yourself, and more open to what’s ahead.

You’re allowed to want more. You’re allowed to dream bigger. And you’re allowed to enjoy creating a life you love.

The eye movement work

How this works

I guide you through reflection, writing, and focused eye movements to explore the feelings, memories, and subconscious beliefs behind the patterns that hold you back—including the ways you may find yourself self-sabotaging, even when you want to move forward.

The eye movements can help bring you into a light, trance-like state, where memories and emotions that are usually outside your conscious awareness may begin to surface. We gently connect with these experiences while moving the eyes, helping to soften and release the emotional charge they carry.

I like to think of it as defrosting emotions that got stuck in time, while creating space to shift the beliefs we formed from those experiences.

Sometimes deep material can emerge, so we work gently, staying connected to your body and the present moment. You're always in control and can pause at any time.

The themes we explore

A theme each month. Space for what unfolds.

We meet four times a month on Wednesdays, for 90 minutes on Zoom. Each month has a theme to guide our work, with room for what emerges along the way.

Themes we may explore include:

  • Abundance, money, and receiving—our beliefs around earning, asking for more, and allowing support, love, and ease into our lives.
  • Love and relationships—attachment patterns, closeness, boundaries, and the ways we relate to ourselves and others.
  • Self-worth, shame, and imposter syndrome—feeling enough, being seen, and trusting what we have to offer.
  • Family and inherited patterns—the roles, expectations, and beliefs we grew up with and still carry.
  • Safety and survival mode—exploring the need to stay in control and making room for more inner safety and trust.
  • Perfectionism and overworking—the pressure to get everything right, please others, or work hard to deserve rest and success.
  • Triggers and emotions—meeting familiar reactions and the feelings underneath them with more awareness and care.
  • Creativity and following our dreams—making space for expression, inspiration, and the courage to follow what matters to us.

The themes are connected and may overlap or return in different ways. The focus of each month will unfold as we go, with space to respond to the group.

What can open up

More freedom to feel, create, love, and live.

As the work unfolds, you may feel more relaxed and clear in everyday life, with more trust in yourself—in your relationship with money, in your relationships with others, and in the choices you make. Breakthroughs can take many forms.

  • More calm, clarity, and self-trust. Feeling more at ease in yourself, hearing what you need, and trusting your choices around money, relationships, and everyday life.
  • Emotional release. Space to meet feelings you’ve carried for years, with the possibility of feeling lighter and less held by the past.
  • A different relationship with your triggers. Exploring what gets activated beneath a strong reaction, with more room to pause and choose how you respond.
  • More freedom in love. Working with attachment patterns, fear of rejection, people-pleasing, and the ways you protect yourself from closeness—with more room to set boundaries and say no with less guilt.
  • Creative breakthroughs. Making room for ideas, expression, and the work you want to bring into the world, while meeting the fear of being seen or getting it wrong.
  • New possibilities in work and money. Exploring self-doubt, perfectionism, overworking, and the beliefs that shape what you allow yourself to ask for and receive.
  • A deeper sense of safety and worth. Building a kinder relationship with yourself, with more space for boundaries, rest, support, and trust.

For me, abundance includes all of this: feeling more at home in yourself, being able to love and create more freely, and having the space to receive and enjoy your life.

Is this space for you?

This is for you if...

You’ve already done some therapy, coaching, somatic work, or other emotional healing, and you’re looking for a space to go deeper. You bring a foundation of body awareness and practices to support yourself.

  • You have some awareness of your body and emotional state. You can notice when you’re starting to check out or feel disconnected, and you have ways to ground yourself and come back to your body.
  • You’re open to feeling a range of emotions. Tears, emotional release, and feelings such as anger can arise, including anger you’ve held back for a long time. You don’t need to be comfortable with every feeling, but you have some experience staying present with emotions and recognizing when you need to pause.
  • You have practices to support yourself between sessions. You know some ways to work with emotions, rest, and integrate, and you’re willing to ask for additional support when you need it.
  • You’re willing to explore without needing every detail to make sense immediately. Experience with subconscious work is helpful. An image, feeling, or association may come up before you understand it; there’s room to be curious and work with what’s present without forcing an explanation.
  • You’re ready to meet patterns that feel stuck. Whether in relationships, creativity, work, money, or your relationship with yourself, you want space to explore more deeply.

This is not for you if...

  • You’re completely new to personal development or emotional healing. This space builds on previous experience; it is not an introduction to inner work.
  • You don’t yet have ways to notice disconnection and return to your body. Building a foundation of body awareness and grounding first will help you come to this work with more support.
  • You’re not ready to be with emotions such as sadness or anger. Emotional release can be part of the work. You can always pause, but some willingness to meet what you feel is important.
  • You need every image or feeling to make sense before exploring it. This work asks for some openness to uncertainty, curiosity, and letting understanding unfold.

Frequently asked questions

About the membership & the work.

How does the monthly membership work?

The Abundance Container is an ongoing women-only membership at $200 USD per month, billed monthly. We meet four times a month on Wednesdays at 8pm Israel time for 90-minute Zoom sessions. Each month has a different theme, with room for overlap as we explore the connections between them.

What if it doesn’t feel right for me?

After your first session, if you don’t connect with the work or feel it isn’t for you, contact me to request a refund of your first monthly payment.

How do the monthly themes work?

Each month has a theme to guide our four sessions. The focus will unfold as we go, drawing on areas such as abundance, relationships, self-worth, creativity, and emotional patterns. Themes may overlap or return, because they often share the same roots.

How do we use the eye movements?

I guide you through reflection, writing, and focused eye movements to explore the feelings, memories, and subconscious beliefs behind your patterns.

The eye movements can help bring you into a light, trance-like state, where memories and emotions that are usually outside your conscious awareness may begin to surface. We gently connect with these experiences while moving the eyes, helping to soften and release the emotional charge they carry.

What might I feel during a session?

You may notice memories, emotions, or sensations in your body as we work. Tears can come, and there may be moments of emotional release. You might also experience something quieter. There is no particular response you need to have.

I guide you to stay connected to what you feel, rather than only talking about it. You can move at your own pace and pause whenever you need to.

I like to think of it as defrosting emotions that got stuck in time, while creating space to shift the beliefs we formed from those experiences.

What might I notice after my first session?

Some women tell me they feel lighter afterward, even when they can’t quite explain what has changed. When the work is new, that unfamiliar feeling can bring questions: “Why do I feel different? What just happened?” You don’t have to make sense of everything straight away.

As you become more familiar with the work, you may begin to notice shifts in everyday life: how you respond to a familiar situation, what feels less charged, or how you relate to yourself. Everyone’s experience is different, and there is no set timeline.

What if an image or feeling comes up that doesn’t make sense?

When working with the subconscious, you may notice an image, sensation, feeling, or association without knowing why it’s there. You don’t need to decode it or turn it into a clear story before we can explore it.

We can stay curious about what you notice and how it feels in your body, letting the process unfold at your pace. Sometimes a connection becomes clearer afterward; sometimes you can work with a feeling without finding an explanation. An image doesn’t have to be taken literally or treated as proof of something that happened.

Experience with this kind of exploration is helpful. You’re invited to trust your own pace and boundaries, and you can always pause or say that something doesn’t feel right.

Do I need to know where a pattern began?

You don’t need to arrive with everything figured out. We begin with a theme and the feelings it brings up. Memories or associations may emerge along the way, and we work gently with what is present for you.

What if strong emotions come up?

Sometimes deep material can emerge, so we work gently, staying connected to your body and the present moment. You're always in control and can pause at any time.

You can choose how much to share and move at your own pace. Please listen to what you need and let me know if you need support.

Give yourself space afterward.

Even when the work feels gentle, touching deep material can leave you feeling tender. Please leave yourself time to rest and integrate. If you need more support, you can book a separate 1:1 session with me.

What happens after you apply?

  1. Apply — about 10 minutes. Fill out the Google application form to tell me a little about yourself.

  2. Receive your confirmation. You’ll get an email confirming I’ve received your application, and I’ll review it personally.

  3. Let’s have a quick call. If we’re a good match, I’ll contact you by WhatsApp or email to arrange a 10-minute call, answer your questions, and get you registered.

  4. Join the group. Once you’re registered, I’ll add you to our WhatsApp group, where you’ll receive the session Zoom link. I’ll also send a Google Calendar or Zoom invitation so you can add the sessions to your calendar.
